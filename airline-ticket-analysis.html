Case Study

Airline Ticket Price Analysis Dashboard

This project explores airline ticket pricing and flight operations using Microsoft Excel. The objective was to identify pricing patterns, travel behaviour and operational trends through data cleaning, PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers and KPI reporting.

Dataset

  • 300,153 flight records
  • 12 variables
  • Airlines
  • Source & Destination Cities
  • Flight Duration
  • Travel Class
  • Ticket Prices

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel PivotTables PivotCharts Slicers Dashboard Design Business Analysis

Data Cleaning

Removing Duplicate Records

The dataset was checked for duplicate records to ensure accurate reporting. Removing duplicates prevented repeated observations from affecting the analysis.

Column Addition

Three columns Price Category, Flight Type and Duration category were added and they helped through the calculation process.

Calculations

Excel Formulas

Excel formulas were used to prepare the dataset and create calculated fields required for the dashboard and PivotTables.

Pivot Tables

Fig 1

Figure 1 shows a Flights by Airline Pivot table used to identify which airline operated the highest number of flights. Average Ticket price by Airline was for comparing ticket prices across different airlines. Flights by Source city helped to analyse departures across major cities.

Fig 2

Figure 2 shows Average ticket price by flight which compared ticket prices between Economy and Business Class. it also shows Average price by flight which showed that connecting flights are more expensive than direct flights.

Interactive Dashboard

The final dashboard combines KPIs, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and slicers into an interactive report that enables users to explore airline pricing trends, travel behaviour, and operational performance.

Business Recommendations

  • Offer incentives for customers who book flights further in advance to encourage early bookings.
  • Increase marketing efforts for Business Class passengers due to their higher average ticket prices.
  • Allocate additional operational resources to Mumbai since it records the highest departures.
  • Continue monitoring airline pricing strategies to remain competitive while maintaining profitability.
