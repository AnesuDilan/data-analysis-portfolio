Case Study

Airline Ticket Price Analysis Dashboard

This project explores airline ticket pricing and flight operations using Microsoft Excel. The objective was to identify pricing patterns, travel behaviour and operational trends through data cleaning, PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers and KPI reporting.

Dataset

  • 300,153 flight records
  • 12 variables
  • Airlines
  • Source & Destination Cities
  • Flight Duration
  • Travel Class
  • Ticket Prices

Business Questions

  • Which airline operates the most flights?
  • Which airline has the highest average ticket price?
  • Which source city records the most departures?
  • Does booking earlier reduce ticket prices?
  • Are direct flights more expensive than connecting flights?

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel PivotTables PivotCharts Slicers Dashboard Design Business Analysis

Data Cleaning

Removing Duplicate Records

The dataset was checked for duplicate records to ensure accurate reporting. Removing duplicates prevented repeated observations from affecting the analysis.

Creating Additional Columns

Three new columns — Price Category, Flight Type, and Duration Category — were created to simplify analysis and support dashboard calculations.

Calculations

Excel Formulas

Excel formulas were used to calculate key metrics and create fields required for PivotTables, charts and dashboard KPIs.

Pivot Tables

Figure 1

This PivotTable analyses flights by airline, average ticket price by airline, and departures by source city to identify travel trends and airline performance.

Figure 2

This PivotTable compares ticket prices by travel class and flight type, showing that connecting flights generally cost more than direct flights.

Interactive Dashboard

The final dashboard combines KPIs, PivotTables, PivotCharts and slicers into a single interactive report, allowing users to explore airline pricing trends, travel behaviour and operational performance.

Key Insights

  • Vistara operated the highest number of flights.
  • Business Class tickets had significantly higher average prices.
  • Mumbai recorded the highest number of departures.
  • Ticket prices generally decreased when bookings were made further in advance.

Business Recommendations

  • Offer incentives for customers who book flights further in advance to encourage early bookings.
  • Increase marketing efforts for Business Class passengers due to their higher average ticket prices.
  • Allocate additional operational resources to Mumbai since it records the highest departures.
  • Continue monitoring airline pricing strategies to remain competitive while maintaining profitability.
