Power BI Case Study

Cruise Revenue Management Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed from a Microsoft SQL Server analysis of cruise booking data. The dashboard transforms raw reservation records into a live view of revenue, occupancy, guest spending and customer satisfaction across cruise lines and ships.

Business Problem

Cruise operators generate large volumes of booking and guest data across multiple ships, routes and package types, making it difficult to track performance using static reports alone. Without an interactive reporting layer, spotting which cruise lines are over- or under-performing, where occupancy is slipping, and which packages drive the most guest spending takes far longer than it should.

The objective of this project was to turn the cleaned booking dataset into a Power BI dashboard that lets management monitor revenue and satisfaction in real time, compare performance across cruise lines, and support pricing and capacity decisions with data rather than intuition.

Project Overview

This project is the second half of an end-to-end analytics workflow that began in Microsoft SQL Server. The booking data was first cleaned, validated and explored using SQL — CTEs, CASE statements, aggregate and date functions — before being imported into Power BI for data modelling, DAX measures and dashboard design.

The final report gives a single, filterable view of revenue, occupancy, guest spending and satisfaction, so the same questions answered with SQL queries in the first stage of this project can now be explored interactively by anyone on the management team, not just someone comfortable writing SQL.

Analytics Workflow

This project demonstrates the complete analytics lifecycle by combining Microsoft SQL Server with Microsoft Power BI to transform raw booking data into decision-ready business intelligence.

1. SQL Data Preparation

The cruise reservation dataset was imported into Microsoft SQL Server, where data quality checks, cleaning and exploratory analysis were performed. SQL queries answered the initial business questions and prepared a reliable dataset for reporting.

2. Power BI Development

The cleaned SQL dataset was imported into Power BI, where Power Query handled data transformation, DAX measures were built for revenue and occupancy KPIs, and interactive visuals were designed around the business questions.

3. Business Intelligence

The completed dashboard lets management monitor revenue and satisfaction by cruise line and ship, identify high- and low-performing routes, and support pricing and capacity decisions with live, filterable data.

Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to help management answer key business questions such as:

  • Which cruise line is generating the highest total revenue?
  • How does occupancy vary across ships and routes?
  • Which packages drive the highest average guest spending?
  • How does customer satisfaction compare across the fleet?
  • Which cruise lines are performing above the revenue average?
  • How do bookings trend over time, and where are the seasonal peaks?

Dashboard

Key Insights & Business Value

  • Royal Caribbean generated the highest overall revenue, indicating strong market performance relative to other cruise lines in the dataset.
  • Occupancy rates varied significantly across routes, highlighting clear opportunities to improve capacity utilisation on underperforming sailings.
  • Premium packages recorded the highest average guest spending, pointing to an opportunity to expand premium offerings.
  • Booking activity showed clear seasonal trends, with peak demand occurring during holiday periods.
  • Certain cruise lines consistently scored above average on customer satisfaction, independent of revenue performance — a signal worth investigating for what those operators are doing differently.

Business Recommendations

  • Increase marketing activity during low-demand months to lift occupancy on underperforming routes.
  • Expand premium package offerings to capture more of the higher guest-spending segment.
  • Review the practices of top-performing cruise lines and apply relevant lessons to lower-performing routes.
  • Track satisfaction alongside revenue when evaluating operational performance, rather than relying on revenue alone.
  • Continue using the interactive dashboard alongside the underlying SQL analysis to support ongoing demand forecasting and pricing decisions.
