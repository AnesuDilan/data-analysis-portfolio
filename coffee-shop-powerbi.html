Power BI Case Study

Coffee Shop Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed from a Microsoft SQL Server analysis to monitor sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance and business trends. The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable insights that support strategic business decisions.

Business Problem

Coffee shop managers generate thousands of sales transactions every day, making it difficult to monitor performance using spreadsheets or static reports. Without an interactive reporting solution, identifying sales trends, best-selling products, peak trading hours and underperforming areas becomes time-consuming.

The objective of this project was to develop an executive Power BI dashboard that enables management to monitor key performance indicators in real time, identify business opportunities and support data-driven decision-making.

Project Overview

This project forms part of an end-to-end data analytics workflow that combines Microsoft SQL Server and Microsoft Power BI. The sales data was first cleaned, validated and analysed using SQL before being imported into Power BI for data modelling, DAX calculations and interactive dashboard development.

The final dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of revenue, transactions, product performance and time-based sales trends through interactive visualisations, KPI cards and slicers, enabling users to explore the data and answer key business questions efficiently.

Analytics Workflow

This project demonstrates the complete analytics lifecycle by combining Microsoft SQL Server with Microsoft Power BI to transform raw sales data into meaningful business intelligence.

1. SQL Data Preparation

The dataset was imported into Microsoft SQL Server where data quality checks, cleaning and exploratory analysis were performed. SQL queries answered key business questions and prepared a reliable dataset for reporting.

2. Power BI Development

The cleaned SQL dataset was imported into Power BI where Power Query was used for data transformation, DAX measures were created for KPIs, and interactive visualisations were designed to support business decision-making.

3. Business Intelligence

The completed dashboard enables management to monitor business performance, identify trends, evaluate product sales and make informed operational and strategic decisions through interactive reporting.

Dashboard Development Process

The dashboard was developed using a structured analytics workflow that began with SQL data preparation and concluded with an interactive Power BI report. Each stage ensured the data was accurate, reliable and transformed into meaningful business insights.

1. SQL Data Preparation

The raw coffee shop sales dataset was imported into Microsoft SQL Server, where data types were validated, data quality checks were performed and business questions were answered using SQL queries. This ensured that the dataset was clean and suitable for reporting.

2. Data Import & Power Query

The cleaned SQL dataset was imported into Power BI. Power Query was used to verify data types, prepare the model and ensure the dataset was ready for analysis and dashboard development.

3. DAX Measures

Custom DAX measures were created to calculate key performance indicators including Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value and other metrics used throughout the dashboard.

4. Dashboard Design

Interactive KPI cards, charts, slicers and navigation were designed to provide management with an intuitive reporting experience. Consistent colours, layouts and formatting were applied to improve readability and user experience.

5. Data Visualisation

Multiple visualisations including bar charts, column charts and line charts were created to communicate sales trends, product performance and time-based patterns in a clear and meaningful way.

6. Business Intelligence

The completed dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence, enabling decision-makers to monitor performance, identify opportunities and support strategic planning.

Key Insights & Business Value

The dashboard uncovered several important business insights that can help management improve operational efficiency, optimise product performance and support strategic decision-making.

  • Peak sales occurred during morning trading hours.

    Morning periods consistently recorded the highest transaction volumes. Understanding peak demand enables management to optimise staff scheduling, reduce customer waiting times and improve service quality during busy periods.

  • Coffee products generated the highest revenue.

    Coffee products were the strongest revenue contributors across all product categories. This insight supports better inventory planning, pricing strategies and promotional campaigns focused on high-performing products.

  • Sales performance varied across store locations.

    Comparing store performance allows management to identify top-performing branches, share best practices and investigate factors affecting lower-performing locations to improve overall business performance.

  • Sales trends changed throughout the year.

    Monthly sales patterns highlighted periods of high and low demand. Recognising these trends assists with inventory forecasting, staffing plans and seasonal marketing campaigns.

  • Product categories contributed differently to total revenue.

    Not all product categories performed equally. This insight enables the business to refine its product mix, discontinue underperforming items and focus investment on the products that generate the greatest return.

Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis, the following recommendations could help improve business performance and support long-term growth.

  • Increase inventory levels for high-performing coffee products to minimise stock shortages during periods of peak demand.
  • Schedule additional staff during the busiest trading hours to improve customer service and reduce waiting times.
  • Evaluate pricing and promotional strategies for lower-performing products to increase sales and improve profitability.
  • Analyse operational practices at top-performing stores and implement successful strategies across other locations where appropriate.
  • Use monthly sales trends to improve inventory planning, budgeting and seasonal marketing initiatives.
  • Continue using interactive Power BI dashboards alongside SQL analysis to monitor business performance and support data-driven decision-making.
